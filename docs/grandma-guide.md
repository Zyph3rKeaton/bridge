# Bridge Scoring Simple Guide

This app helps you keep score for a bridge game.

## Put It On Your Mac

1. Open the file Keaton sends you.
2. If a window opens with **Bridge Scoring** and **Applications**, drag **Bridge Scoring** onto **Applications**.
3. Open **Applications**.
4. Double-click **Bridge Scoring**.
5. If your Mac says it cannot open the app, call Keaton.

## Open The App

1. Double-click **Bridge Scoring**.
2. Wait for the home screen to appear.
3. If something looks wrong, close the app and open it again.
4. If it still does not work, call Keaton.

## Start A New Game

1. Click **New Session**.
2. Leave the usual choices alone unless you need to change them.
3. Click **Create Session**.
4. When the app says the game was created, click **OK**.

## Enter A Score

1. Click **Board Entry**.
2. Pick the board you are scoring.
3. Tap the contract number.
4. Tap the suit.
5. Tap **Doubled** or **Redoubled** only if needed.
6. Tap who played the hand.
7. Tap how many tricks were made.
8. Check the score shown on the screen.
9. Click **Save & Next Board**.

## See The Results

1. Click **Results**.
2. Look at **Final Standings** to see the current scores.
3. Click **Export Text** if you want to save a copy.

## Fix A Mistake

1. Click **Results**.
2. Find the row with the mistake.
3. To remove the row, click the red **X**.
4. To change a number, double-click the number and type the right one.
5. If you are not sure what to change, call Keaton before deleting anything.

## Use A Photo

Typing scores by hand is the safest way.

If Keaton has set up photo reading for you:

1. Click **Scan**.
2. Choose a clear photo of the score sheet.
3. Click **AI Parse & Import**.
4. Check the results afterward.

If the photo reader gets confused, type the score by hand instead.

## Close The App

When you are done, close the window like any other app.
