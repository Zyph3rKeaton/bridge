# Bridge Scoring Simple Guide

I made this app to help you keep score for a bridge game.

## Put It On Your Mac

1. Open the file I sent you.
2. If a window opens with **Bridge Scoring** and **Applications**, drag **Bridge Scoring** onto **Applications**.
3. Open **Applications**.
4. Double-click **Bridge Scoring**.
5. If your Mac says it cannot open the app, call me.

## Open The App

1. Double-click **Bridge Scoring**.
2. Wait for the home screen to appear.
3. If something looks wrong, close the app and open it again.
4. If it still does not work, call me.

## Turn On Photo AI

Do this one time.

1. Click **Settings**.
2. Click the **OpenAI Key** box.
3. Paste the key I sent you.
4. Click **Save Key**.
5. When it says **Saved. Photo AI is ready.**, you are done.

## Start A New Game

1. Click **New Session**.
2. Leave the usual choices alone unless you need to change them.
3. Click **Create Session**.
4. When the app says the game was created, click **OK**.
5. Click **Events** to see the boards for the game.

## What The Main Buttons Mean

Use these buttons from the top of the app.

1. **Home** takes you back to the first screen.
2. **New Session** starts a new bridge game.
3. **Events** shows the current game and all of its boards.
4. **Scan** reads a score sheet photo.
5. **Results** shows standings and board results.
6. **Board Entry** lets you type one board by hand.
7. **Settings** is where the Photo AI key is saved.

## Find The Boards

Use **Events** when you want to work board by board.

1. Click **Events**.
2. Look under **Boards**.
3. Find the board number you want.
4. Click **Scan** on that board.
5. The app will open the photo screen for that board.
6. After importing, click **Results** to check the board.

## Enter Scores With A Photo

This is the main way to enter scores.

1. Click **Events**.
2. Find the board you want.
3. Click **Scan** on that board.
4. Choose a clear photo of the score sheet.
5. Click **AI Parse & Import**.
6. Wait while the app reads the photo.
7. Click **Results**.
8. Check that the scores look right.

## Take A Good Photo

A clear photo helps the app read the score sheet correctly.

1. Put the score sheet flat on a table.
2. Make sure the whole sheet is in the picture.
3. Use good light.
4. Hold the camera still.
5. If the photo looks blurry, take another one.
6. If the app reads something wrong, fix it on **Results**.

## See The Results

1. Click **Results**.
2. Look at **Final Standings** to see the current scores.
3. Scroll down to **Board Results** to check each board.
4. Click **Export Text** if you want to save a copy.
5. Do not click **Clear Results** unless you want to erase the scores and start over.

## Fix A Mistake

1. Click **Results**.
2. Find the row with the mistake.
3. To remove the row, click the red **X**.
4. To change **Made** or **Down**, double-click the number and type the right one.
5. Press **Enter** or click away to save the change.
6. If you are not sure what to change, call me before deleting anything.

## Type A Score By Hand

Use this only if the photo reader misses something.

1. Click **Board Entry**.
2. Pick the board you are scoring.
3. Tap the contract number.
4. Tap the suit.
5. Tap **Doubled** or **Redoubled** only if needed.
6. Tap who played the hand.
7. Tap how many tricks were made.
8. Check the score shown on the screen.
9. Click **Save & Next Board**.

## Board Entry Details

These are the choices on the hand-entry screen.

1. **Select Board** chooses which board you are scoring.
2. **Level** is the contract number, from 1 to 7.
3. **Suit** is Clubs, Diamonds, Hearts, Spades, or No Trump.
4. **Doubled** and **Redoubled** are only used when the contract was doubled.
5. **Declarer** is the player who played the hand: N, E, S, or W.
6. **Tricks made** is the total number of tricks won, from 0 to 13.
7. **Calculate Score** shows the score without moving to the next board.
8. **Save & Next Board** saves the score and moves forward.

## If Photo AI Does Not Work

Try these steps before typing everything by hand.

1. Click **Settings**.
2. Make sure it says an OpenAI key is saved.
3. Go back to **Scan**.
4. Try a clearer photo.
5. If it still does not work, call me.

## Close The App

When you are done, close the window like any other app.
