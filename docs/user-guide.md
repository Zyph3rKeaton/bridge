# User Guide (Grandma Quick Start)

Welcome! This tablet app helps you keep score for bridge.

## Big Buttons, Simple Steps

1. Tap "New Session".
2. Choose the game type: Duplicate Pairs, Teams, Chicago, or Rubber.
3. Follow the steps on screen. The app will set common defaults.
4. Enter results per board with the big suit buttons.
5. Tap "Save & Next Board".

You can always tap "Undo". The app saves as you go. No internet required.

## Helpful Tips
- "Pass-out" and "No Play" buttons are available on the board screen.
- Rankings update live. Tap "Rankings" from the bottom bar.
- Need to fix something? Tap "Director" and follow the prompts.

## Accessibility
- Large text. High contrast. Works with screen readers.
- Use the settings to turn on bigger text or high-contrast mode.
